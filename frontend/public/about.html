What is This Website?

This Pet Project is a digital pet game where you take care of a virtual companion. You can feed your pet, play with it, let it sleep, clean it, give it treats, or simply chat with it.

How the Game Works

Take a picture of an object, which will then be converted into a digital pet. You can chat with your pet, and it will respond based on its mood and stats. Your pet has four main stats: Hunger, Happiness, Energy, and Health. Your actions affect these stats, and your pet’s mood changes depending on how well you care for it.

Actions You Can Take

Chat: Talk to your pet and see how it responds based on its mood.
Feed: Lowers hunger and improves happiness.
Play: Increases happiness but uses energy.
Sleep: Restores energy.
Pet: Makes your pet feel loved.
Clean: Improves health and comfort.
Treat: Gives a happiness boost.

Created For
